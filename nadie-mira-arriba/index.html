How to play

Keyboard and gamepad

A/D or arrow keys: move. Space: jump. W/↑: look up. Z: crouch. X: shoot. C: grenade. F/V: melee. Q: cycle weapons. Esc: pause.

Gamepad: A jumps, B crouches, RT shoots, RB throws a grenade, LT looks up, X melees, Y cycles weapons, Start confirms / pauses.

Select PLAY with Enter, Space, A or Start. Your browser may need an interaction to enable audio.
